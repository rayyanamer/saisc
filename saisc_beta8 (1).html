Academic Preparation for Intermediate Scholars

The intermediate years provide an important foundation for further study. SAISC Gojra offers science and computing programmes with an emphasis on subject learning, classroom instruction and preparation for board examinations.

Campus Environment and Facilities

The college operates separate boys’ and girls’ campuses in Gojra. Facility details and photographs should be reviewed and confirmed by the administration.

01

A Closed Building Campus

A secure closed building structure that maintains student safety, controlled entry, and a quiet atmosphere away from outside noise.

02

Computer Laboratory

The computer laboratory supports practical learning for students enrolled in computing-related coursework.

03

Library

Stocked with intermediate textbooks, reference guides, and past board papers for quiet self study.

04

Friendly and Guiding Staff

Supportive coordinators and staff who guide students through daily routines and keep parents informed.
